# CarDex 

CarDex is an interactive automotive platform built for people who are obsessed with cars.

It combines AI-powered car recognition with an automotive catalog and a car guessing game, wrapped in a retro pixel-art inspired interface.

## Features

### AI Car Recognition
Upload an image of a car and CarDex uses AI to identify the vehicle and provide relevant information about it.

### Automotive Catalog
Explore vehicles and their specifications through an interactive automotive catalog.

### Car Guessing Game
Test your automotive knowledge through trivia, silhouettes, and car identification challenges.

### Interactive Experience
A visual interface inspired by retro automotive games, pixel art, motorsport graphics, and classic car culture.
